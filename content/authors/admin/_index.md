Junyoung Kwon is an Assistant Professor in the Division of Materials Science and Engineering at Hanyang University. He received his Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering from Yonsei University in 2020, advised by Prof. Gwan-Hyoung Lee, and conducted visiting research at Seoul National University and Columbia University during his doctoral studies.

Before joining Hanyang University, he spent over six years as a researcher at Samsung Advanced Institute of Technology (SAIT), where his work on wafer-scale 2D semiconductor integration was published in *Nature*, *Nature Materials*, *Nature Electronics*, and *Nature Nanotechnology*.

His research focuses on integrating two-dimensional semiconductors into next-generation logic devices, with an emphasis on monolithic 3D integration, contact engineering, and wafer-scale process development for future semiconductor technologies.
