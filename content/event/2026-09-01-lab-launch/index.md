SIM Lab (Semiconductor Integration Materials Laboratory) officially launches on **September 1, 2026**, at the Division of Materials Science and Engineering, Hanyang University, under the direction of Prof. Junyoung Kwon.

The lab will focus on 2D semiconductor materials and their integration into next-generation logic and memory devices, with particular emphasis on monolithic 3D integration and contact engineering.

We welcome inquiries from prospective graduate students and collaborators. Please visit our [People](../../people/) or [Contact](../../contact/) page for more information.
